# BA Portfolio — Inventory Mismatch Dispute Resolution
### E-Commerce Supply Chain | Business Analyst Case Study

## 📋 Project Overview
This portfolio documents a complete Business Analyst case study 
investigating an inventory mismatch dispute between SnapMart 
(seller) and QuickKart (e-commerce platform).

**Problem:** 500 units dispatched by SnapMart, only 320 received 
by QuickKart — 180 units missing, cause unknown.

**Role:** Business Analyst — investigate, analyze, and recommend resolution.
